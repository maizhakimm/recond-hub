Some cars are never on a showroom floor in Malaysia: a specific Ferrari spec, a limited-run Lamborghini, a McLaren in the colour you have always wanted. That is what **custom import** (private sourcing) is for.

## Step 1: define the car

Start with the essentials:

- **Make, model and year range**
- **Colour and options**: exterior, interior, wheels, carbon packs, lift system
- **Mileage and history** requirements
- **Budget** and **timeline**

The more precise the spec, the faster the search.

## Step 2: sourcing

We search dealer networks, auctions and private collections in **right-hand drive** markets such as Japan, the UK, Hong Kong, Singapore and Australia. Each candidate is checked for:

- Service history and ownership
- Independent inspection
- Accident and finance checks
- Import eligibility (age, specification)

You approve the car before any commitment.

## Step 3: approvals and taxes

Used cars are imported under an **Approved Permit (AP)**. The main cost components are:

- **Import duty**, which depends on where the car was made and trade agreements
- **Excise duty**, the largest component for big engines, rising steeply with engine capacity
- **Sales tax**

All are calculated on the car's customs-assessed value. For a V8 or V12, total taxes can be more than the car itself costs overseas. We give you a full landed-cost estimate before you commit.

## Step 4: shipping and clearance

The car is shipped (container or RoRo), insured in transit, then cleared through customs at Port Klang or another port.

## Step 5: inspection and registration

In Malaysia the car goes through **Puspakom** inspection, then **JPJ** registration, road tax and insurance. We also arrange ceramic coating, PPF or detailing if you want.

## Step 6: handover

Delivered to your door, registered in your name.

## How long does it take?

| Stage | Typical time |
| --- | --- |
| Search and approval | 1 to 4 weeks |
| Purchase and export paperwork | 1 to 3 weeks |
| Shipping | 2 to 6 weeks |
| Clearance, Puspakom and JPJ | 2 to 4 weeks |

Allow **8 to 16 weeks** in total.
