Walk into any car lot in Malaysia and you will hear three words: **new**, **recond** and **used**. They are not the same thing, and the difference affects price, loan, insurance and resale.

## What is a recond car?

A **recond** (reconditioned) car is a used car that has been imported, usually from Japan or the United Kingdom, inspected and reconditioned, and then **registered in Malaysia for the first time**. On paper you become the first Malaysian owner.

Recond cars are imported under an **Approved Permit (AP)**. Most are young, typically a few years old, because import rules favour newer cars.

## What is a used car?

A **used** car (kereta terpakai, second-hand) has already been registered and driven in Malaysia. The registration card (geran) will show the previous owners.

## Side-by-side

| | Recond | Used (local) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Registered in Malaysia before | No | Yes |
| History document | Auction sheet (Japan) | Service record, previous owners |
| Typical age | 1 to 5 years | Any |
| Mileage | Usually low | Varies |
| Loan tenure | Often up to 9 years | Often shorter |
| Spec | Japan/UK spec, often higher trim | Malaysian spec |
| Price | Higher than used, lower than new | Lowest |

## Why people choose recond

- **Models you cannot buy new here**, like the Alphard SC, Harrier Z Leather or Civic Type R at the right price.
- **Higher specification**: pilot seats, sunroofs and premium audio are common in Japan-market cars.
- **Low mileage with proof**, thanks to the auction sheet.
- **First registration in your name**, which helps resale.

## What to watch out for

- Always ask for the **auction sheet** and check it. Our guide shows [how to read a Japanese auction sheet](/guide/how-to-read-japanese-auction-sheet).
- Check what the **warranty** covers and for how long.
- Japan-market cars may need a **radio frequency converter** and some menus may be in Japanese. Good dealers sort this before handover.
- Budget for **road tax and insurance** on the full value. See [road tax and insurance costs for recond cars](/guide/road-tax-and-insurance-recond-cars).

## So which should you buy?

If you want a specific high-spec model, low mileage and a long loan, recond is usually the best value. If your budget is tight and you are happy with an older car, a well-kept used car can make sense.
