Price and monthly instalment are only part of the cost. Every year you also pay **road tax** (cukai jalan) and **insurance**. Here is how to estimate both.

<Callout title="Check before you buy">
Rates below are for guidance. JPJ and insurers update their rules, and hybrids and EVs have their own treatment. Confirm with JPJ's official calculator or your agent before committing.
</Callout>

## Road tax: based on engine size

Road tax for a privately owned petrol car in Peninsular Malaysia depends on engine capacity (cc):

| Engine capacity | Annual road tax |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 1,000 cc | RM 20 |
| 1,001 to 1,200 cc | RM 55 |
| 1,201 to 1,400 cc | RM 70 |
| 1,401 to 1,600 cc | RM 90 |
| 1,601 to 1,800 cc | RM 200 + RM 0.40 per cc above 1,600 |
| 1,801 to 2,000 cc | RM 280 + RM 0.50 per cc above 1,800 |
| 2,001 to 2,500 cc | RM 380 + RM 1.00 per cc above 2,000 |
| 2,501 to 3,000 cc | RM 880 + RM 2.50 per cc above 2,500 |
| Above 3,000 cc | RM 2,130 + RM 4.50 per cc above 3,000 |

### Examples

| Car | Engine | Road tax per year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mercedes-Benz C200 | 1,496 cc | RM 90 |
| Toyota Harrier 2.0 | 1,986 cc | about RM 373 |
| Honda Civic Type R | 1,996 cc | about RM 378 |
| Toyota Alphard 2.5 | 2,494 cc | about RM 874 |
| Toyota Alphard 3.5 | 3,456 cc | about RM 4,182 |

This is why the 2.5 litre is by far the most popular Alphard and Vellfire.

## Insurance: based on the car's value

Comprehensive insurance is priced on the **sum insured** (the car's market value), plus your age, location and claims history.

- A rough starting point is **2.5% to 3.5%** of the sum insured before discounts.
- Your **No Claim Discount (NCD)** can cut the premium by up to 55%.

For a RM 200,000 recond SUV that is roughly RM 5,000 to RM 7,000 before NCD, or about RM 2,250 to RM 3,150 with 55% NCD.

### Transferring your NCD

NCD belongs to you, not the car. When you buy a recond car you can transfer the NCD from your current car. Ask us and we will arrange it with the insurer.

### Useful add-ons

- **Windscreen** cover
- **Special perils** (flood, landslide), strongly recommended
- **Legal liability of passengers**, important for MPVs

## Your first-year total

For a RM 250,000 Alphard 2.5 in Selangor with 55% NCD, budget roughly:

- Road tax: about RM 874
- Insurance: about RM 2,800 to RM 3,900
- Total: around **RM 3,700 to RM 4,800** in year one
