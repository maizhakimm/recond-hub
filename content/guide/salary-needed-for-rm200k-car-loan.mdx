A RM200,000 recond SUV or MPV is a big purchase. Before you fall in love with a Harrier or Vellfire, it helps to know what the bank will look at.

## Step 1: the monthly instalment

Car loans in Malaysia (hire purchase) use a **flat rate**:

- Loan = price − down payment
- Total interest = loan × rate × years
- Monthly = (loan + total interest) ÷ months

For a RM200,000 car with **10% down**, **3.0% flat** and **9 years**:

- Loan: RM 180,000
- Interest: RM 180,000 × 3% × 9 = RM 48,600
- Total payable: RM 228,600
- Monthly: RM 228,600 ÷ 108 = **RM 2,117**

## Step 2: your DSR

Banks divide all your monthly commitments by your income. This is the **Debt Service Ratio (DSR)**. Our calculator treats below 60% as likely, 60 to 70% as borderline, and above 70% as unlikely. Every bank sets its own limits and many look at net income, so treat these as a guide.

<Callout title="Real-world rule of thumb">
DSR is only one test. For a car at this price, most banks are most comfortable when the instalment is no more than about a third of your gross income, which points to a salary of RM 6,000 to RM 8,000.
</Callout>

## How to improve your chances

1. **Pay down credit cards** before applying. Banks count the minimum payment.
2. **Put more down.** Each extra RM 10,000 cuts about RM 118 a month over 9 years.
3. **Choose a longer tenure** to lower the instalment (you pay more interest overall).
4. **Show stable income**: payslips, EPF statements and, for business owners, bank statements and Form B.
5. **Apply to several banks at once.** We do this for you.
