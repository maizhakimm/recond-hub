## The generations

### AH30 (2015 to 2023)

The AH30 is the Alphard most Malaysians know. The **facelift** (from 2018) added Toyota Safety Sense, sequential indicators and a bigger grille. For recond buyers in 2026, the sweet spot is a **2021 to 2023 facelift 2.5**, still young enough for a 9-year loan.

### AH40 (2023 onward)

The new generation has a stiffer body, a much better ride and a more luxurious cabin. It is also far more expensive, and supply of recond units is limited.

## Which variant to buy

| Variant | Who it suits |
| --- | --- |
| 2.5 S | Budget buyers who want the Alphard badge |
| 2.5 S C Package | Best value: pilot seats and most of the comfort |
| 2.5 SC | The favourite: power pilot seats, more equipment |
| 3.5 Executive Lounge | Maximum luxury, higher road tax |
| Hybrid | Better fuel economy, check battery health |

Most families are happiest with the **2.5 SC** or **S C Package**. The 2.5 engine also keeps road tax reasonable. See our [road tax guide](/guide/road-tax-and-insurance-recond-cars).

## What affects the price

- **Grade and interior score** on the [auction sheet](/guide/how-to-read-japanese-auction-sheet)
- **Mileage**: under 30,000 km carries a premium
- **Colour**: pearl white and black hold value best
- **Options**: sunroof, twin moonroof, JBL, rear entertainment
- **Registration year**: unregistered cars can be registered in your name at any time
